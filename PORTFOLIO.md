# Portfolio

I am a systems researcher. My subject is autonomous AI systems: how they are designed, how they behave in operation, how they fail, and how people keep oversight of them. This page lists the research lines at QNFO first, then the systems work that led to them. Every QNFO paper is prepared with an AI-assisted research pipeline; I am responsible for its content.

---

## Research lines

### 1. Autonomous systems in operation

**Question:** what does a long-running autonomous AI system actually deliver, at what cost, and how does it fail?

QNFO is both the lab and the subject. Its research pipeline runs on Cloudflare as an autonomous system of 44 live workers that draft, referee, revise, version, publish and correct papers, keep a public errata trail and check citations, with standing objectives, health checks and a failure ledger. The library is at [papers.qnfo.org](https://papers.qnfo.org).

| Work | What it is |
|:-----|:-----------|
| [Operating the Quniverse Fleet](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23079905) | Objectives, successes, failures and roadmap of the system, with its failure ledger |

### 2. Oversight and legibility of AI-assisted work

**Question:** how can the output of an AI system say clearly what it does not know, so that people can oversee it?

### 3. The cost of a correct answer (JPCUB)

**Question:** what does a correct answer cost the whole system, including the retries and checking that agentic AI needs?

| Work | What it is |
|:-----|:-----------|
| [Joules-per-Solution for Stochastic and Agentic Inference](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21945415) | The metric applied to frontier and agentic LLMs |
| [The Joules-per-Solution Metric](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21637028) | Definition, measurement protocol and anti-gaming rules |

**Status:** the metric and protocol are published. The figures are estimates from published specifications; no hardware measurement has been made yet. Earlier estimates covered quantum platforms; that line is no longer a focus.

### 4. Theory (exploratory)

Ultrametric and adelic approaches to computation and information, published openly in the library. These are hypotheses, not results. I do not lead with them until a falsifiable test has been run.

## The systems work behind it

### Transportation networks and systems planning (2004 to 2011)

My first role in transportation, as a GIS Analyst at Cross County Connection TMA, was spatial data and transportation networks. As a Systems Planner at the Delaware Valley Regional Planning Commission (2005 to 2008) and a consultant at IBI Group (2008 to 2011) I built and ran transportation network models.

### FHWA national travel model (2011 to 2015)

At the U.S. Federal Highway Administration I specified the national Tour-Based Model System, a simulation of long-distance passenger travel across the United States, and directed the contractor team that built it. I co-directed the $10M U.S. DOT National Household Travel Survey and presented its data needs at the 2011 TRB National Household Travel Survey workshop. I managed a $1.5M+ federal research portfolio as a certified Contracting Officer's Representative.

### Machine learning and analytics (2015 to 2016)

At Deloitte Consulting I delivered analytics and machine-learning engagements for federal and public-sector clients, and co-authored a 2016 TRB paper on updating the National Household Travel Survey with American Community Survey data. I was then a Senior Data Scientist at The Advisory Board Company.

### Predictive data products (2021 to 2023)

At Epsilon (Publicis Groupe) I managed data products built on predictive machine-learning models, and I was then Platform Data Product Manager at iManage.

### AARP Livability Index (2016 to 2021)

A national decision-support system that scores U.S. neighborhoods on seven categories: housing, neighborhood, transportation, environment, health, engagement and opportunity.

- **My role:** Product Manager and Senior Methods Advisor, AARP Public Policy Institute. I owned the product across multiple public releases and co-authored the Livability Index 2018 report.
- **Scale:** 50+ data sources combined into one scoring framework, at census-tract and block-group level, with user-adjustable category weights and API access for researchers.
- **Use:** municipal planners, community development organizations and grant-makers. The Index and its method are cited in 20+ academic and policy studies.

### Empowering Change (2023 to 2024)

A nonprofit I founded and led from incorporation to public launch. It built an LLM legal-navigation tool for self-represented litigants with three parts: document drafting, step-by-step procedural guidance, and plain-language translation of legal terms.
