## Programs and products

### AARP Livability Index (2016 to 2021)

A national platform that scores U.S. neighborhoods on seven categories: housing, neighborhood, transportation, environment, health, engagement and opportunity.

- **My role:** Product Manager and Senior Methods Advisor, AARP Public Policy Institute. I owned the product across multiple public releases and co-authored the Livability Index 2018 report.
- **Scale:** 50+ data sources combined into one scoring framework, at census-tract and block-group level, with user-adjustable category weights and API access for researchers.
- **Use:** municipal planners, community development organizations and grant-makers. The Index and its method are cited in 20+ academic and policy studies.

### FHWA national travel forecasting (2011 to 2015)

At the U.S. Federal Highway Administration I managed a $1.5M+ federal research portfolio as a certified Contracting Officer's Representative. I led technical components of the national Tour-Based Model System, a simulation of long-distance passenger travel in the United States, and presented its data needs at the 2011 TRB National Household Travel Survey workshop.

### Empowering Change (2023 to 2024)

A nonprofit I founded and led from incorporation to public launch. It built an LLM legal-navigation tool for self-represented litigants with three parts: document drafting, step-by-step procedural guidance, and plain-language translation of legal terms.

### QNFO research system (2024 to now)

QNFO is an independent research imprint. Its pipeline runs on Cloudflare as an autonomous system of 44 live workers that draft, referee, revise, version, publish and correct papers, keep a public errata trail and check citations. The library is at [papers.qnfo.org](https://papers.qnfo.org); every record has a DOI on Zenodo. What the system delivers, what it costs and where it fails are written up in [Operating the Quniverse Fleet](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23079905).

## Research lines

### 1. Energy-honest computing (JPCUB)

**Question:** what does a correct answer cost in energy, for the whole system, including cooling, control and error correction?

| Work | What it is |
|:-----|:-----------|
| [The Joules-per-Solution Metric](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21637028) | Definition, measurement protocol and anti-gaming rules |
| [Error Correction Is a Landauer Machine](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22261547) | The thermodynamic floor of quantum error-correction overhead |
| [JPCUB Competitive Landscape v2.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21821767) | System-level estimates for 17 quantum platforms, from published specifications |
| [Joules-per-Solution for Stochastic and Agentic Inference](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21945415) | The same metric applied to frontier and agentic LLMs |

**Status:** the metric and protocol are published. The platform figures are estimates from published specifications; no hardware measurement has been made yet.

### 2. Epistemics of AI-assisted science

**Question:** how can an AI-assisted paper say clearly what it does not know?

| Work | What it is |
|:-----|:-----------|
| [The Universal Ignorance Audit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21901984) | A fifteen-question method for stating the limits of a piece of research |
| [Knowing What We Do Not Know](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21901983) | Ignorance auditing, AI-generation detection, and lessons from an AI-assisted pipeline |
| [Epistemic Legibility in AI-Assisted Science](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22026592) | Ignorance auditing as a governance tool for peer review |

### 3. Autonomous research operations

**Question:** what does an autonomous research system actually deliver, at what cost, and how does it fail?

[Operating the Quniverse Fleet](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23079905) reports objectives, successes, failures and the roadmap, with a failure ledger.

### 4. Theory (exploratory)

Ultrametric and adelic approaches to computation, information and physics, published openly in the library. These are hypotheses, not results. I do not lead with them until a falsifiable test has been run.
